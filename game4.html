Crownmerge: Hero Defense

Merge · Defend · Rebirth

Merge heroes, defend the wall and rebirth for crowns in this idle tower defense!

Crownmerge: Hero Defense is a casual game you can pick up any time: combine heroes into stronger ones, hold the wall against waves of enemies, and rebirth to earn crowns.
